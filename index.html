About

Hi, I'm Akshansh, a product designer working with startups and growth-stage teams. With 8+ years of experience, I help founders turn signups into active, retained users by designing data-informed UX that improves activation, retention, and revenue. I work end-to-end across research, flows, prototyping, and UI to ship products that drive measurable business outcomes.

Experience

I work across contract and full-time roles, depending on the stage and needs of the product. My experience spans healthcare, fintech, travel, edtech, gaming, B2B SaaS, and consumer products used across the US, Europe, India, and Japan.
